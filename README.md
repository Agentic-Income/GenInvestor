# GenInvestor

A local-first investor research workspace. Every number should show its source.

**Status:** research preview.

## What it is

GenInvestor is a workspace for testing investment theses against dated evidence rather than isolated numbers. Two ideas shape the design:

- **Evidence ledger.** Figures and claims are recorded with their source, date, and assumptions, so a result can be traced back to the material behind it.
- **Claims audit.** A claim is only as strong as the evidence attached to it. The workspace is built to make missing or weak support visible.

The preview is read-only. It does not connect brokerage accounts, draft orders, or move money. It reports research and simulations, not recommendations.

## Research preview

Explore the preview: <https://geninvestor-research.vercel.app>

The preview presents published experiments with their source data and assumptions, a passive baseline for comparison, and the limits of each result.
